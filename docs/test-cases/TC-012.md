Linked criteria: F-05 AC-01 ([F-05](../features/F-05-profile-settings.md))

Priority: P2

## Preconditions
- A registered user, signed in (tests create one through the API).
## Steps
1. Open /settings.
2. Enter a new bio.
3. Click "Update Settings".
4. Open the user's profile.
## Expected result
The app goes to /, and the profile shows the new bio.
